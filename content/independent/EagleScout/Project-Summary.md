# Alum Creek Park Renovation

Eagle Scout service project — Cameron Shellenbarger, Troop 200 BSA.

## Overview

I planned and led volunteer crews renovating the putt-putt course and nature-center area at Alum Creek Campground. We refreshed the gardens, improved the course, and repainted the outdoor checkerboard for campers to enjoy.

## Planning and leadership

I coordinated with the park and my troop, recruited volunteers, purchased materials, and led multiple crews working on different tasks. I adjusted assignments to keep teams focused and checked their progress and safety.

## Changes and challenges

Staining the shelter and moving the gaga ball pit were removed from the plan to avoid disturbing holiday campers. Overgrown flowerbeds made their boundaries difficult to identify, and excess materials required additional cleanup.

## Project totals

- 19 participants, including me.
- 128 total project hours across planning and execution by all participants.
- $453.10 spent on materials, funded from my personal savings.

## Outcome

The renovation improved the recreation area for campers. The park's assistant manager was pleased with the completed work. Leading the project helped me build confidence in coordinating teams and solving problems.
