Personal Code of Ethical Conduct

1. Avoid actions that could hurt others.
2. Respect people's rights and privacy.
3. Be honest and take responsibility for my mistakes.
4. Treat people fairly regardless of their background or beliefs.
5. Only access accounts, files, and systems I have permission to use.
6. Keep private information confidential.
7. Speak up when I see something harmful or unethical.
8. Keep my promises and follow through on my responsibilities.
9. Give credit for other people's work.
10. Keep learning and use feedback to improve my decisions.
