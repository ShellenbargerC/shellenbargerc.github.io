This research exercise focused on recommending security measures for a small dental office. I examined HIPAA technical safeguards and compared them with PCI DSS requirements.

Security Measures I Recommended
---
- Give each employee a unique account and prohibit account sharing.
- Limit access to patient information based on job responsibilities.
- Establish emergency access procedures for authorized staff.
- Configure automatic logoff after inactivity.
- Encrypt stored patient information and use TLS for transmission.
- Record and review access logs and changes to patient records.
- Use strong passwords and multifactor authentication.
- Use integrity checks to detect unauthorized changes.


HIPAA and PCI DSS comparison
---
HIPAA protects patient health information, while PCI DSS focuses on payment account data. Both involve controlling access, protecting sensitive information, and monitoring activity.


Small business considerations
---
