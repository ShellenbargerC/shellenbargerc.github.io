In Spring 2026 I competed in the **National Cyber League (NCL) Team Game** on the **Cyber Skyline** platform with my team, **Royal Flush**. We earned a **Platinum** medal, finishing **229th out of 3,634 teams** — top 6.3%, or the 78th percentile overall.

## Results

- **2,395 / 3,000** points
- **83.4%** completion
- **161 correct** answers out of 243 attempts (66.3% accuracy)
- **0 skipped, 0 hints** — every point earned unassisted

## About the competition

The National Cyber League is a defensive and offensive cybersecurity competition run on the Cyber Skyline platform. Team Game challenges span cryptography, password cracking, web application security, network traffic analysis, log analysis, enumeration and exploitation, and open-source intelligence (OSINT). Over a multi-day event, teams work through as many challenge categories as they can, with points awarded per solve and no penalty bigger than a wrong answer costing an attempt.

## What I took away

- Working under a shared team clock changes how you triage: we split categories by strength, doubled up on the high-value challenges, and parked the ones eating attempts.
- Accuracy matters as much as speed. Our 66.3% accuracy came from agreeing as a team to sanity-check answers before submitting, since every wrong attempt was a wasted shot.
- Not taking a single hint across 83.4% completion is the result I'm most proud of — it means the solves were real, not guided.
